Simulate various subpixel patterns.

## Properties

[proptable]
Subpixel
Type|Subpixel pattern.
Density|Pattern scale.
Scene Scale|Scale original surface.

Effect
Size|Subpixel size.
Blur|Blur subpixel.

Ridge
Ridge|Add wave effect per each subpixel.
Ridge Amount|The amount of wave per subpixel.
Ridge Intensity|The darkening effect.

Render
Intensity|Brightness of the output.
Noise|Apply random noise to the output.

Flicker
Flicker|Animate subpixel flickering.
[/proptable]
