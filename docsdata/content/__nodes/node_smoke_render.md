Update and render the smoke simulation to surface.

## Properties

[proptable]
Domain
Auto Update|Update smoke before rendering.
Update Step|Updating step, impact simulation speed.

Render
Volume Color|Smoke color over density.
Density Remap|Remap density to fit the <junc Volume Color> gradient.
Transparent|Render density to alpha channel instead of greyscale.
[/proptable]
