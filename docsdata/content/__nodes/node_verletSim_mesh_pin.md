Pin verticies in a mesh to be unaffected by gravity.

## Properties

[proptable]
Mode
Mode|Pin mode\n
 - **Override**: Pin selected vertices and unpin unselected vertices.\n
 - **Pin**: Pin selected vertices and leave other vertices the same.\n
 - **Unpin**: Unpin selected vertices and leave other vertices the same.

Target
Source|Vertices selection type.
[/proptable]
