Spawn grain from sorces.

## Properties

[proptable]
Spawn
Type|Spawn grain every frame (stream), or once on trigger (trigger).
Period|Frame period in stream type.

Shape|Spawn shape.
Spacing|(For fixed geometry shape) Grain invert density. Larger value means less dense. Can be lower than 1 for hard solid object.

Simulation
Sleep on Spawn|Make grain sleep (not affected by gravity) on spawn.
[/proptable]
