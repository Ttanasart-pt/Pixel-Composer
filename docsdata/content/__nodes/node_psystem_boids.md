Simulate boid movement to particle.

## Properties

[proptable]
Separation
Separation|Add separation force, pushing nearby particle away from each other.
Radius|Radius to begin separating particle.
Influence|Strength of the pushing force.

Alignment
Alignment|Make particle align themselves with the surrounding particles.
Radius|Alignment radius.
Influence|Alignment force.

Grouping
Grouping|Pull nearby particle toward each other to create grouping effect.
Radius|Pull radius.
Influence|Strength of the pulling force.

Follow
Follow|Make particle attempt to follow a single point.
Point|Point target.
[/proptable]
