Simulate smoke path. A smoke path consists of two phases, wave and spiral.

## Properties

[proptable]
Spawn
Type|Spawn type.
Amount|Amount of smokes.

Initial State
Lifespan|Smoke liftspan. Will also control smoke size.
Direction|Smoke direction.

Wave
Life Range|Range of lifespan dedicated to wave movement.
Wave|Amount of wave over the wave phase.
Shape|Wave shape.
Frequency|Wave frequency.
Amplitude|Wave size.

Spiral
Life Range|Range of lifespan dedicated to the spiral movement (can intersect with wave).
Spiral|Amount of spiral over the spiral phase.
Velocity|Spiral velocity.
Flip|Randomized spiral fliping.

Offset
Offset|Add random position offset.

Animation
Animation|Enable smoke animation.
Anim Shift|Random animation starting frame.
[/proptable]
