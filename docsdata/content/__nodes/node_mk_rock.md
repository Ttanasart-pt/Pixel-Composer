Generate rock pile.

## Properties

[proptable]
Pile
Origin|Pile position.
Pile Scale|Scale factor for each subsequenct rock in the pile.

Scatter
Amount|Amount of rocks to generate.
Scatter|Scatter distance.

Rock
Shape|Base shape.
Size|Rock size.
Ratio|Height to width ratio.
Depth|Rock height.
Shape Scale|Scale rock per height.

Layer
Shear|Shift rock position per layer.

Cut
Chance|Chance for drawing extra shape to cut away the rock.
Shape|Cut shape.
Scale|Cut scale.

Nugget
Nugget|Draw randomized nugget inside the rock.
Chance|Chance for spawning nugget.
Shape|Nugget shape.
Span|Nugget position away from rock center.
[/proptable]
