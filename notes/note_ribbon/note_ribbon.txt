Draw fixed direction ribbon from a path.

## Properties

[proptable]
Path
Path|The path to draw the ribbon.
Range|Path range.
Loop|Add extra connect from begin to the end to ensure loop.
Sample|Amount of points to sample from the path.
Invert|Reverse path direction.

Ribbon
Size|Ribbon size.
Direction|Ribbon direction.
Thickness|Ribbon thickness. Thick ribbon can have artifact when path intersected.


[/proptable]
