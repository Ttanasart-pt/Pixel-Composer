Apply transformation on particle using gradient of a vector field (RG texture).

## Properties

[proptable]
Vector
Intensity|Vector amplitude.
Midpoint|Greyscale level to set as origin [0,0] point.
Overflow|When particle is outside surface range, whether to sample as repeating texture or sample at the edge.
Rotate|Rotate vector.
[/proptable]
