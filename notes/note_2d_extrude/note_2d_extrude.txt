Extends surface in one direction.

## Properties

[proptable]
Extrude
Angle|Extrusion angle
Distance|Extrusion distance
Shift|The ratio where the orinal surface will be positioned.
Wrap|Wrap extrude surface to the oppsite side of the surface when overflowed.

Transform
Rotation Modulate|Rotate surface as its extruding.
Scale Modulate|Scale surface as its extruding.

Highlight
Highlight|Apply different color to the edge of the extrusion.
Width|Width of the highlight.
Color|Color of the highlight.
[/proptable]
