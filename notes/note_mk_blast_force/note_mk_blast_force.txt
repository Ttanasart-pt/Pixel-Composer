Apply force to blast particles.

## Properties

[proptable]
Blast
Blast|Blast object.
Mask|Filter object type to be affected.

Selection
Selection|Apply effect only to selected area.

Fixed Curve
Fixed Curve|Apply force based on particle's own lifespan.
Move Curve|Strength curve over lifespan.
Life Range|Life span range to apply to curve.
[/proptable]
