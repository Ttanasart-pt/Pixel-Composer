Target a single bone and apply rotation to it. That rotation then propagate to the children with frame delay.

## Properties

[proptable]
Stagger
Amount|How many children depth will the tranformation propagated.
Frame|The amount of frame dalay for each propagation.
Loop|Whether to loop back the data when reach the end of the animation.

Rotation
Rotation|Rotation amount.
Stiffness|The amount of rotation lost per child/layer.
Intertia|Reaction speed for each children to reach the parent rotation.
[/proptable]
