Generate a stylized panel texture.

## Properties

[proptable]
Panels
Area|Area to generate the panel.
Panel Pattern|A text defining type of panel division available for the randomizer to pick from.
Min Iteration|Limit the range of iteration count.
Max Iteration|Limit the range of iteration count.
Min Size|Minimum panel size (relative to original dimension) before stoping panel subdivision.
Padding|Add padding before subdividion.
Panel Colors|Method for selecting panel color.

Split
Inset Range|Range of possible inset padding.
Split Ratio|Range of possible split ratio.
Split Width|Add extra width on the split line.
Split Alternate|Force next split to be in the opposite axis as the previous one.

Split Draw|Whether to draw the split line.
Split Length|Ratio of split line to draw.

Shapes
Shape Pattern|A text defining shape of the panel available for the randomizer to pick from.
Corner|Add corner to rectangular shape.
Corner Shuffle|Randomize corner position.
Pipe Size|Size of the pipe shape.

Highlight
Highlight|Add edge highlighting.

Glow
Use Glow|Add glow to the output.
[/proptable]
