Destroy fluid particles in a selected area. 
### Properties

[proptable]
Domain|Fluid domain to operate

Mask
Shape|Shape of the area
Position|Middle point of the area to destroy particles
Radius|Radius of the area for circle shape
Size|Size of the area for rectangle shape

Destroy
Chance|The probability that a particle in the area will be destroyed (0 being none and 1 being all particle in area)
[/proptable]
