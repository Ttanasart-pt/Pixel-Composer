Separate each detached region of a surface into array of surfaces.

## Properties

[proptable]
Shape
Mode|Whether to use transparent pixel to separate region or black pixel.
Blank|Whether to include blank pixel as one of the output.
Diagonal|Whether to include diagonally connected pixel as one region.

Output
Override Color|Override output with solid color.
Crop|Crop output to fix only region.
[/proptable]
