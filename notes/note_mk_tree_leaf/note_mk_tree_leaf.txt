Generate leaves.

## Properties

[proptable]
Spawning
Position|Leaf position relative to orignal branch.
Chance|A chance that any given original branch will spawn leaves.

Spawning > Scatter
Amount|Leaves amount.
Distribution|Leaves distribution.

Spawning > Settings
Apply to Property Curves|Set the 'Over Branch' property to use 'Position' range or total range.

Direction
Spread|Leaf direction away from the original branch direction.
Reflect|Flip leaf direction over the original branch.
Reflect Order|Order of reflection.\n
 - **Even**: Flip every even leaf.
 - **Odd**: Flip every odd leaf.
 - **Random**: Flip randomly.

Direction > Gravity
Gravity|Strength of the gravity.
Override|Override default gravity direction defined in <node Node_MK_Tree_Inline>.

Grouping
Whorled|Spawn multiple leaves at one point at a different direction.
Whorled Angle|Shift angle of all leaves in a group.
Whorled Span|Total rotation amount per group.

Shape
Shape|Leaf shape.
Size|Leaf size.

Growth
Grow Delay|When use with <node Node_MK_Tree_Grow>, offset growth amount.
[/proptable]
