Gradient node draw gradient on to the surface. Visit <node gradient_out/> for a node that output a gradient data.

<img gradient_types/>

## Properties

[proptable]
Gradient
Gradient|The gradient to draw.
Shift|Shift the gradient keys by a fix amount. <img gradient_shift/>
Scale|Scale the gradient. <img gradient_scale/>
Loop|Loop the keyframe back when overflowing. <img gradient_loop/>

Shape
Type|Types of the gradient. <img gradient_types/>
Angle|<span class="inline-code">Linear</span> and <span class="inline-code">Radial</span> types only. The angle of the pattern.
Radius|<span class="inline-code">Circular</span> type only. Radius of the pattern.
Senter|Center point of the pattern.
Uniform ratio|For non-square output, whether to follows the surface aspect ratio or use uniform 1:1 ratio. <img gradient_uniform_ratio/>

Rendering
Level|Apply brightness level adjustment.
Curve|Applt brightness curve adjustment.
[/proptable]
