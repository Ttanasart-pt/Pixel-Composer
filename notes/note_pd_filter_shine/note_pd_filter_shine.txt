Apply shine effect on the surface.

## Properties

[proptable]
Shine
Axis|Shine main axis.
Progress|Shine position relative to total movement.
Shines|Shine pattern, in alternate shine-empty order.
Scale|Shine scale.
Slope|Shine slope, relative to main axis.
Flip|Invert shine direction.

Offset
Offset|Offset shine position per pixel.
Range|Offset range.

Rendering
Colors|Set color per shine.
Blend Mode|Shine blend mode.
Keep Alpha|Keep original surface opacity.
[/proptable]
