Generate blast effect. The effect consists of layers of circular group of shaded circles. Most ranged properties in this node refers to the amount 
from the first (bottom) layer to the last (top) layer.

## Properties

[proptable]
Settings
Layer Interpolation|Interpolation curve between each layer.
Anim Interpolation|Animation progress curve.

Spawning
Layers|Amount of layers.
Frame|Frame offset.

Spawning > Source
Position|Blast position.
Area|Scatter area.
Radius|Group radius.

Spawning > Group
Group Size|Amount of blast per group.
Scale|Group scale.

Movement
Type|Whether to animate blast by frame of sampling data from animation curve.
Speed|Movement speed.
Friction|Speed reduction per frame.
Direction|Movement direction.
Gravity|Apply gravity.

Rotation
Rotate|Initial angle.

Size
Size|Blast size per layer.
Spawn Size|Randomize size per blast.
Aspect Ratio|Blast shape.

Spiral
Spiral Use|Draw spiral inside each blast.

Decay
Decay|Destroy blast from the middle.
[/proptable]
