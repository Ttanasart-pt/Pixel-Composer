Scale surface. A simplified transform node with option for fitting surface to a fix dimension.

## Properties

[proptable]
Scale
Mode|Scale mode:\n
 - **Upscale**: Multiply dimension by a number.\n
 - **Scale to Size**: Scale surface to fit fix dimension.
Fit Mode|Scaling method for Scale to Size mode.\n
 - **Stretch**: Scale surface non-uniformly to fix the <junc Target Dimension>.\n
 - **Keep Aspect Minimum**: Keep the aspect ratio of the original surface, scale to fit inside the <junc Target Dimension>.\n
 - **Keep Aspect Maximum**: Keep the aspect ratio of the original surface, scale to cover the whole <junc Target Dimension>.
Move Atlas|When inputting Atlas data. Whether to scale the atlas position as well.
[/proptable]
