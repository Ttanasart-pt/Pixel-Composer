Simulate rain effect.

## Properties

[proptable]
Shapes
Shapes|Rain particle shape.

Lifespan
Lifespan|Define particle lifespan. Unlike most particle node the rain drop contains no lifespan data in itself.
Size over Lifetime|Modulate size over lifespan.
Alpha over Lifetime|Modulate opacity over lifespan.

Effect
Density|Particle amount.
Direction|Rain direction.
Velocity|Raindrop speed.

Render
Fade Alpha|Fade rain particle.

Ground
Ground|Make raindrop hit the ground.
Ground Start|Ground Y position.

Ripple
Ripple|Render ripple when raindrop hit the ground.
Rip. Amount|Amount or ripple created per raindrop.
Rip. Lifespan|Ripple lifespan.
Rip. Radius|Ripple size.
Rip. Delay|Delay between each ripple.
[/proptable]
