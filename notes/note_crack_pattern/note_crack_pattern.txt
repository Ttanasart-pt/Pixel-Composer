Generate crack pattern.

## Properties

[proptable]
Origin
Pattern|Crack pattern
Amount|Crack amount.
Both Side|Whether to extends crack in the opposite side.

Pattern
Segments|Amount of segments.
Scale|Crack scale.

Crack
Width|Crack amplitude, maximum distance a crack can varience from the original direction.
Branch|Chance for a crack to spawn another crack.
Angle|Range of the angle where the new crack generate.
Radial|Draw a radial line connecting multiple crack lines.

Trim
Trim|Trim crack line for animation.

Thickness
Thickness|Crack rendering width.

Rendering
Color|Base color
Branch Blend|Multiply color per branch.
Texture|Draw texture along crack line.
[/proptable]
