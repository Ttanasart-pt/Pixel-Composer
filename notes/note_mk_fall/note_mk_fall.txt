Simulate leaf falling effect.

## Properties

[proptable]
Spawn
Area|Leaf spawn area.
Amount|Leaf amount.

Physics
Gravity|Gravity force.
Wind|Apply uniform force to all leaf.

Swing
Swing frequency|How often the leaf change swing direction.
X Swing|Amount of swing in X axis.
Y Swing|Amount of swing in Y axis.

Leaf
Shape|Leaf shape.

Ground
Ground|Make leaf stop falling when hitting the ground level.

Twist
Twist|Make the leaf move in spiral path.
[/proptable]
