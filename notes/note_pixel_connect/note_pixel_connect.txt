Draw solid line connecting all solid pixels in the surface. 

## Properties

[proptable]
Connection
Max Distance|Maximum pixel distance, have high impact on performance.
Resolution|Amount of line to check per pixel.

Rendering
Blend Type|Whether to color the line based on closed pixel, or smoothly blend between two pixels.
Blend Mode|Blend mode between line color and <junc Color>.
Color|Color to blend.
[/proptable]
