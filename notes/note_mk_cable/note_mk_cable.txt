Generate cables hanging between two points.

## Properties

[proptable]
Anchors
Type|Cable origin:\n
 - **Fix Points**: Draw cables between two points.\n
 - **Fix Point Array**: Draw cables connecting from one point to another in point array.\n
 - **Path Anchor**: Draw cables connection series of points sampled from a path.\n
 - **Path Sample**: Draw cable from path.\n
 - **Areas**: Draw cables between 2 areas.
 
Cable
Amount|Amount of cables.
Gravity|Direction of the gravity.
Tension|How much does gravity effect cable.
Segments|Amount of segment per cable.

Swing
Swing|Animate path swinging.
Amplitude|Swing amplitude.
Frequency|Swing speed.

End Swing
End Swing|Swing the end of the cable around the gravity direction.
[/proptable]
